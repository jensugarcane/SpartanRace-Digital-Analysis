# Spartan-Race-Facebook-Content-Performance-Analysis

Analyzed 393 Facebook posts from Spartan Race (30 Aug – 15 Oct 2016), built an interactive Excel dashboard, and delivered data-driven recommendations on content format, posting frequency and scheduling to lift engagement.

## Project Objectives

* Assess the performance of Spartan Race's current Facebook activities.
* Clean and structure post-level data (post type, content pillar, day of week, hour, time of day) for analysis.
* Calculate key metrics such as engagement per post, comment rate, sharability rate and posts per day.
* Visualize insights through an interactive Excel dashboard with slicers (Post Type, Content Pillar, Time of Day).
* Recommend a strategy to optimize content and enhance engagement.

## Tools Used

* Excel – For data cleaning, PivotTables, calculated metrics, charts and the interactive dashboard with slicers
* Power BI – For building interactive dashboards and visualizing key business insights
## Dataset

* **Source:** Spartan Race Facebook page post data (case study dataset)
* **Time range:** 30 Aug – 15 Oct 2016 (47 days)
* **Dataset details:** 393 posts, including post type (photo, video, link, status, event), content pillar, posting date/time, and engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares).

## Outcome

* The dataset has been cleaned, categorized and enriched with the metrics needed for content performance analysis.
* The final dashboard enables analysis across **Post Type, Content Pillar, Day of Week, Hour of Day and Time of Day**.
* Findings were turned into a **3-part action plan** (format, frequency, scheduling) and a sample content calendar.

## Key Insights Presented

* **Format:** Photo and video make up ~90% of posts. Photo earns the most engagement per post (945 vs 734 for video, **1.3x more**).
* **Untapped formats:** Status posts have a 37.9% comment rate but only 2 posts were published; link posts average 288 engagements per post, more than double status and event.
* **Frequency:** Spartan posted 8.4 posts/day vs 1.5 recommended (up to 25 posts on 1-Oct). 1-Oct (25 posts) averaged 664 engagements per post vs 1,092 on 2-Oct (17 posts), so more posts did not mean more engagement. 6–10 posts/day looks optimal.
* **Day of week:** Saturday has the highest engagement from only 46 posts, while Wednesday gets 57 posts but is among the weakest days.
* **Hour of day:** 15:00 is the peak hour (39,473 engagements); 01:00 returns the most per post (1,679) and 12:00 returns 1,215 per post.

## Recommendations

1. **Diversify content format** – Rebalance photo and video, test status posts, and curate high-value link posts.
2. **Invest in quality, not quantity** – Limit daily posts to 6–10 to protect content quality and organic reach.
3. **Refine post scheduling** – Add posts on Saturday, add slots at 12:00–13:00 and 01:00, keep 15:00 and 18:00, and cut back 14:00, 16:00–17:00 and 23:00.
